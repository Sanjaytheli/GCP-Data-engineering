step1: go to below url 
https://docs.cloud.google.com/sdk/docs/install-sdk

Note: better to install telegram application in your personal laptop also 

step2: scroll down ==> click on windows tab ==> click on Download the Google Cloud CLI installer. ==> after that it will download a file into your downloads folder (GooglCloudSDKINstaller)

step3: go to downloads folder ==> double click on this .exe file ==> agree terms and condition ==> single user/multi user ==> next / next / next / install (straight forward installation) ==> in next window it will show your green status bar (from here it will take 10mins or 15 mins or 20 mins based on your network speed)
Note: in this installation process please dont disconnect your internet

==> will start creation of google free trail account 

step1: go to below url in your browser 
https://console.cloud.google.com

step2: we need to login into above url 

scenario1: in your browser already you logged in by using your existing mail id, above url by default will login by using your existing mail id ==> if you want you can change your mail id

scenario2: in your browser if you haven't logged in then above url will ask enter gmail id and pwd ==> enter your mail id and pwd ==> login into above url 

check your mail id 
country ==> India 
agree terms and condition ==> click on agree and continue 

step3: in next window in middle of the screen or at top right side corner you can see "Try for Free" ==> just click on that 

step4: in next window it will ask your personal details and payment details 

contact information ==> + symbol  ==> popup window ==> profile type (individual) ==> given your name + address + city name + pin code + state  ==> save 
Note: credit or debit card whatever address you are having please provide same details here (atleast 60 to 70%)

payment information ==> + symbol ==> click on that ==> in next page credit/debit card or by using UPI/QR code or netbanking ==> here please select credit/debit card ==> in next page card number + expiry date of card + cvv + name ==> save card 

click on "start free" ==> it will ask for payment confirmation ==> confirm ==> enter cvv ==> submit ==> it will ask you to enter OTP details ==> enter that otp whatever you received on your mobile ==> verify 

scenario1: if your payment is going to succeed then in next page it will ask some random questions, just provide random answers ==> submit ==> in next window in middle of the page you can able to see how much free credits your received (28700) / expiry date of your account [Your account is created successfully]

scenario2: might be for some ppl you can able to see error messages as part of your payment 
Reason1: might be your card is invalid

Reason3: your card is valid + your card is having international transaction access + but because of Google payment server issues also you can get error
