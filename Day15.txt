==> read data from customer_lo.json file, after that I want to get total grade as a result.

spark dsl:

from pyspark.sql.functions import sum
customer_lo_df=spark.read.format("json").load("gs://cdebatch5758sparkusecase/source/customer_lo.json")
customer_lo_df.agg(sum("grade").alias("tot_grade")).show()

spark sql:

customer_lo_df=spark.read.format("json").load("gs://cdebatch5758sparkusecase/source/customer_lo.json")
customer_lo_df.createOrReplaceTempView("customer_lo_v")
spark.sql("select sum(grade) as tot_grade from customer_lo_v").show()

==> read data from customer_lo.json file, after that I want to get total grade as a result for every salesman id.

spark dsl:
from pyspark.sql.functions import sum
customer_lo_df=spark.read.format("json").load("gs://cdebatch5758sparkusecase/source/customer_lo.json")
customer_lo_df.groupBy("salesman_id").agg(sum("grade").alias("tot_grade")).show()

spark sql:

customer_lo_df=spark.read.format("json").load("gs://cdebatch5758sparkusecase/source/customer_lo.json")
customer_lo_df.createOrReplaceTempView("customer_lo_v")
spark.sql("select salesman_id,sum(grade) as tot_grade from customer_lo_v group by salesman_id").show()
